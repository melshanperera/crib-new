FAQ

Questions about your credit?

The Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka (CRIB) is the first credit bureau in South Asia, established to collect and provide credit information to lending institutions.

MyReport is your personal credit report that summarizes your credit history, including active facilities, payment behaviors, and overall credit health.

You can request your credit report directly through the CRIB online portal by registering an account, or by visiting our office with valid identification.

If you find a discrepancy, you can raise a dispute directly through the CRIB portal. We will investigate the matter with the respective lending institution.

Credit information is updated monthly by member lending institutions, ensuring your report reflects your most recent financial activities.

Only you and authorized member institutions (like banks) evaluating your credit application can access your CRIB report. It is highly confidential.

Bureau Governance

Leadership, Oversight, and Institutional Integrity at the Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka

Board of Directors

The Management and Affairs of the Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka, as per section 5.1 of the Act is vested in the Board of Directors. Members of the Board as at 01.01.2026.

Mr.K.G.P.Sirikumara

Chairman - CRIB

View Profile

Mr.K.G.P.Sirikumara is the Deputy Governor in charge of Financial System Stability and possesses over 25 years of experience in the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, covering multiple facets of central banking. His professional journey is marked by leadership in policy development, financial system oversight, and institutional reform, with a strong emphasis on safeguarding the stability and integrity of Sri Lanka’s financial sector.

Before his appointment as Deputy Governor, he functioned as an Assistant Governor and was entrusted at various times with responsibilities relating to currency management, public debt, deposit insurance, financial sector resolution, finance, and a range of corporate services. In these roles, he contributed significantly to strengthening institutional processes, enhancing operational governance, and improving the resilience of key financial sector functions.

Mr.K.G.P.Sirikumara has played a central role in advancing major law reforms in the banking and financial sector, financial market infrastructures, capital markets, and in Sri Lanka’s broader framework for combating money laundering and terrorist financing. He has driven the introduction of stronger governance standards within the Central Bank and championed the development of modern enforcement and resolution mechanisms to reinforce financial stability.

He currently serves as the Chairman of the Financial System Stability Committee and the National Payments Council, two of the country’s most important forums for safeguarding systemic stability and advancing payment system modernization.

Beyond his regulatory and policy roles, Mr.K.G.P.Sirikumara has contributed meaningfully to academia and professional education. He holds Master of Laws degrees from Monash University, Australia, and the University of Colombo.

Mrs. R Rukshana S de Silva Jayatillake

Director - CRIB

View Profile

Mrs. R R S de Silva Jayatillake currently serves as the Director of Bank Supervision of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka. Mrs. Jayatillake has over 20 years’ experience in bank supervision and regulation. Prior to her appointment to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka she has served as a Financial Officer at a commercial bank.

She has participated in several local and international training programs and seminars on various aspects of bank supervision and regulation conducted by foreign central banks, Bank for International Settlements, and the Financial Stability Board. Mrs. Jayatillake holds a Master of Arts degree in Financial Economics from the University of Colombo and is an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants, UK.

Mr. Sampath Perera

Director - CRIB

View Profile

Mr. Sampath Perera was appointed Deputy General Manager in February 2024, marking a significant milestone in his career at Bank of Ceylon. With over two decades of experience, Mr. Perera has consistently demonstrated exceptional leadership skill and strategic acumen across various areas within the bank.

Having joined the Bank in 2002 as a Management Trainee, Mr. Perera quickly rose through the ranks, showcasing his proficiency in diverse banking functions. His tenure as Assistant General Manager, spanning more than six years, saw him successfully manage key strategic business units, including Western Province North, Western Province South, Offshore Banking Division, and Corporate Relations Division.

Mr. Perera's international exposure includes a 6-month stint as a Trainee Dealer at Bank of Ceylon London in 2007. His contributions were instrumental in establishing the commercial lending operations at Bank of Ceylon (UK) Ltd.

Mr. Perera holds a B.Sc. (Special) in Business Management from Rajarata University of Sri Lanka, a Master of Business Administration from the Postgraduate Institute of Management (PIM), University of Sri Jayawardenepura, and a Diploma in Banking & Finance.

Mrs. Samanthi Senanayake

Director - CRIB

View Profile

Ms. Samanthi Senanayake is a Legal Professional with over 32 years of experience as an Attorney-at-Law. She also holds a Master of Law (LL.M) from University of Delhi, India. In addition, she has successfully completed professional qualifications in Compliance Aspects and has been awarded with International Diploma in Compliance by the International Compliance Association in collaboration with University of Manchester.

Ms. Senanayake began her legal career as a Legal Intern at People’s Bank and has since gained more than 3 decades of experience working as an Individual Practitioner and as a member of the Corporate Sector. She joined People’s Bank in 2001 as an Assistant Law Officer and carried out her duties as a Law Officer for 9 years.

Presently, Ms. Senanayake serves as an Executive Member of Association of Compliance Officers of Banks – Sri Lanka. She also holds the positions of Compliance Officer of the Bank to Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka (CRIB) and a member of the Information Committee established under the Right to Information Act.

Mr. Naleen Edirisinghe

He has also served on the Boards of Fintrex Finance Limited, Ceylinco Holdings PLC and RIL Property PLC.

Mr. Senarath Bandara

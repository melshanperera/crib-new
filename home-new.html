FAQ

Questions about your credit?

What is CRIB?

The Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka collects and provides credit information to support informed lending and borrowing decisions.

What is a MyReport?

MyReport is your personal credit report, bringing together information about your credit facilities and repayment history.

How can I obtain my credit report?

Register through the MyReport portal, or visit CRIB with valid identification. Explore MyReport.

How can I correct inaccurate information?

Raise a dispute through CRIB’s official channels. CRIB will investigate the information with the relevant lending institution.

How often is credit information updated?

Member lending institutions provide monthly updates to credit information.

Who can access my credit information?

Your credit report is confidential. Access is restricted to you and authorised member institutions.
